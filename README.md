# SimsLife: Minecraft Bedrock Life Simulator

SimsLife is a Minecraft Bedrock plugin that brings the life simulation experience inspired by The Sims into your world. This plugin introduces human NPCs with various roles, social interaction menus, a burglar system, and a craftable burglar alarm to enhance your gameplay. With SimsLife, you can create a vibrant and dynamic town where NPCs live, interact, and even try to burglarize your homes.

## Install

1. Download the SimsLife plugin from the EnderDevelopment marketplace.
2. Place the downloaded JAR file into the `plugins` folder of your Minecraft Bedrock server.
3. Start or restart your server to load the plugin.

## Use

1. **Spawn NPCs**: Use the command `/sim spawn <role>` to spawn human NPCs with different roles such as Mayor or Mailman.
2. **Interact with NPCs**: Right-click on an NPC to open the social interaction menu. Choose from options like Chat, Joke, Argue, and Make Friends to build relationships.
3. **Craft Burglar Alarm**: Use the command `/sim craft alarm` to craft a Burglar Alarm. Place it inside your house to protect your valuables from burglars.
4. **Experience Burglaries**: At night, burglars may spawn and attempt to break into houses. Use the Burglar Alarm to scare them away before they can steal your items.
